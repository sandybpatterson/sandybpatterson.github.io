Recipe

Garlic Olive Oil & Butter Mashed Potatoes

Serves 8

Ingredients

  • 4 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt (for the water)
  • ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, cubed
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 tsp kosher salt (to season, adjust to taste)
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives or rosemary (optional)

Prep (do this first)

About 15–20 min. Buy a little extra so you still have 4 lb after trimming.

  1. Rinse the potatoes to get the dirt off.
  2. Peel them.
  3. Check for bad spots and cut them out:
    • Green patches: cut them away completely; the green is bitter and can upset your stomach. Throw out any potato that’s green all over.
    • Sprouts and eyes: dig them out with the tip of the peeler or a paring knife.
    • Bruises, dark spots, hollow centers: trim them off; the rest is fine.
    • Soft, mushy, wrinkled, or bad-smelling potatoes: throw them out whole.
  4. Cut into 2-inch chunks, all about the same size so they cook evenly.
  5. Put the chunks straight into the pot of cold water so they don’t turn gray.
Make ahead: prep up to a day early. Keep the chunks covered in cold water in the fridge. On the day, drain, add fresh cold water and the salt, and start at step 1.

Steps

  1. Put the prepped potatoes in a large pot, cover with cold water by 1 inch, and add the 2 tbsp salt.
  2. Bring to a boil (10–15 min), then simmer 15–20 min, until a knife slides through with no resistance.
  3. Meanwhile, warm the oil and garlic over low heat for about 10 min, until the garlic is soft and golden, not brown. Mash the garlic into the oil with a fork.
  4. Heat the milk until steaming. Don’t boil it.
  5. Drain the potatoes. Return them to the hot pot over low heat and stir 1–2 min to steam off moisture.
  6. Rice them or mash by hand. Don’t use a blender or food processor, or they’ll turn gluey.
  7. Stir in the butter and garlic oil, then add the warm milk a little at a time until creamy. You may not need all of it.
  8. Season with salt and pepper, taste, and adjust. Top with herbs and a drizzle of olive oil.
Dairy-free: use about ⅔ cup olive oil and no butter. Replace the milk with warm potato cooking water or unsweetened oat milk.

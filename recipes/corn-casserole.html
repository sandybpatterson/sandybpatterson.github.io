Recipe

Classic Corn Casserole

Serves 10 · one 9×13 pan

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) whole kernel corn, drained
  • 1 can (14.75 oz) cream-style corn, not drained
  • 1 box (8.5 oz) corn muffin mix
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×13 baking dish.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, sour cream, and melted butter until smooth.
  3. Stir in both cans of corn and the pepper.
  4. Add the muffin mix and stir just until no dry streaks remain. Don’t overmix, or it bakes up tough.
  5. Pour into the dish and spread it level. If using cheese, scatter it over the top.
  6. Bake 45–55 min, until the top is golden, the edges pull away from the pan, and the center no longer jiggles. A knife in the middle should come out mostly clean.
  7. Rest 10 min before serving. It firms up as it cools.
Make ahead: mix it up to a day early, cover, and refrigerate. Add 5–10 min to the bake time if it goes in cold.

Variations: stir in a diced jalapeño or a 4 oz can of green chiles for heat, or top with crumbled cooked bacon. For a softer, spoonable texture, skip the eggs.

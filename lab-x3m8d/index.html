Redesigning the Orders Tab

Same look as the Prep work redesign. The Orders tab stops being a long list and becomes a live dashboard: how the run is going, what the bot is doing right now, and the board the way the game shows it.

01Today

real screenshot current Orders tab (example orders filled in)

What works

Clear orders, honest counts, and the "working on it now…" line. The redesign keeps all of that.

What holds it back

  1. The big picture is missing. Points per hour, score to the next level, storage and the express state are only in the log.
  2. One long column. The game shows Food Club as three cards side by side; here they're stacked, so the clipboard falls off the bottom.
  3. Every item looks the same. "4/7" and "0/4" have the same weight. You can't see at a glance which card is close to done.
  4. "Needs making" says nothing new. The useful facts would be "cooking, ready in 2m", "growing, 3m" or "blocked: Dairy busy 3 h".
  5. No reward on the card. Points earned and coins on offer aren't shown, even though the bot now measures both.

03What changes

TodayNew design
"updated 4 s ago" lineA stats strip: points per hour, score with a bar and next-level time, storage with a forecast, completed count
"working on it now…" on one itemA Now: bar at the top that says what the bot is doing and where
Food Club orders stackedThree cards side by side, like the game board (they stack on a narrow window). The card being worked on is outlined gold; an express card turns gold
"0 of 2 ready"A progress ring per card, and the closest-to-done card is marked
"needs making"The real state: cooking ⋅ time left, growing ⋅ time left, queued behind N, waiting for an ingredient, held/blocked and why
Plain countsCount plus a thin bar; colour: red = far, amber = on the way, green = done
Clipboard below Food ClubIts own row of cards with a coins badge from the ranking test ("good" / "skip?")
Now / History buttonsA short Recent feed under the boards; the full History stays one click away
Only looks change. Everything shown is something the bot already knows or is learning in the log-only tests (timers, storage, the simulator's forecasts, the coin ranking). No new taps.

04Questions for later

  1. Stats strip: are these the four numbers you want to see first? Swap candidates: gems, coins, "next express usually around…"
  2. Clipboard badges: show "skip?" only once the coin ranking test proves accurate. I'd hide them until then.
  3. One look for every tab: same cards, pills and bars for Orders, Prep work, Log and Settings. Do them one tab at a time, with a mock-up each?

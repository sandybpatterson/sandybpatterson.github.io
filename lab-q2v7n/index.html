Redesigning the Log and Settings Tabs

The last two tabs, in the same look as Prep work and Orders, so the whole window matches.

01The Log tab

real screenshot today

What holds it back

  1. It's written for me, not you. SIFT inliers, card x-positions and tracebacks scroll by. The few lines that matter to you ("order completed +62", "storage full") are buried.
  2. Everything is the same white text. A crash looks exactly like "arrived → station".
  3. Tracebacks take 20 lines for one error, and a normal Stop shows up as one.
  4. The filter box is the only tool, and you have to know what to type.

What changes

  1. Story view (default): only what you care about, in plain words with an icon: orders completed with points, expresses, Prep progress, warnings, problems. Grouped by run, newest first.
  2. Raw view: the full log, coloured by area (Food Club, Prep, simulator, problems in red). Tracebacks fold into one line, and a normal Stop isn't shown as an error.
  3. Chips with counts instead of typing filters: Food Club, Clipboard, Prep, Problems, Tests.
  4. Send logs is unchanged: the file I read stays exactly the same.

Same look everywhere. All four tabs would share the same pieces: rounded boxes, gold section titles, switches, − / + steppers, status pills and ⓘ hovers. Each tab gets a mock-up before it's built.

03Order to build them

  1. Prep work: you already loved it, and it fixes real clutter.
  2. Orders: the tab you look at most.
  3. Log: Story view first, Raw colouring second.
  4. Settings: polish, the smallest job.

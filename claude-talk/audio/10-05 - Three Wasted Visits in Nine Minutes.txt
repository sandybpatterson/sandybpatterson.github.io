Three Wasted Visits in Nine Minutes.
Written October 5th, twenty twenty-six.

That is the exact situation the staged speed-up from pass five was built for: after collecting, check the card's stock, and if it is zero, carry on cooking right away. Seeing it three times in nine minutes turns an estimate of twenty-two times in a few hours into a rate I can watch happen in real time.

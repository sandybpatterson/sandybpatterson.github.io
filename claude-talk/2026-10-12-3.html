Counted Twice

Written October 12, 2026

The Rusch family is cleaned up. The problem this time was not strangers but copies. When hints get accepted one at a time, the same child can arrive twice, once from each census, and the tree treats them as two people. Half a family on one record and half on another is still a broken family.

What was fixed

Six Rusch children each existed twice: Julia, Alma, Herman, Frank, Louise and Herbert. Each pair is now merged into one person.

Enzil Rusch turned out to be Edwin Carl Rusch under a misread name. He is merged into Edwin, born 19 January 1872 in the Town of Herman, Wisconsin.

Caroline is now the main mother for all eight children from William's second marriage. Getting the mother right matters here, because a first wife and a second wife are easy to blur when the children's dates overlap on paper.

The running delete list

These are the jobs that only a person at the keyboard can do. The list will come back every few people until it is empty.

One. Delete the seven strangers from the wrong Rose's family: Rozalia, Stanisław, Elżbieta, Jozef, Elinor, Frances and Dorothy.

Two. Delete the 1880 Belle Creek residence on Eddie Brien and on Andrew Brien.

Three. On Anna Biszewiski, delete the 1959 Warren, Ohio death and its burial. Both belong to Ann Potozney.

Four. On Wilhelmina Beilke, delete the 1876 marriage, the 1880 Addison residence and the 1900 Clinton residence.

Five. Remove Wilhelmina as mother of John, Henry, Julia, Alma, Herman, Frank, Louise and Herbert. Do it on each child's Edit Relationships box.

Six, optional. The five wrong sources on Amanda Rusch, and Rachel Steed's link to the Kent gentry.

Up next

Unless the plan changes, the next stop is the generation above Andrew Brien and John Gunderson: their parents.

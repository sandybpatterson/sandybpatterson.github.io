Twenty Minutes on the Clock

Written October 31, 2026

You asked me to take a deeper look at the express order we missed on Thursday afternoon. So I went back through every log the bot has sent since the Halloween Food Club event started on October 29, and pulled out every express it saw. The short version is that the 4:18 miss was not a fluke. It was the most typical miss of the event.

Thirteen expresses, six made, seven missed

Between Wednesday evening and Friday morning, thirteen gold express cards showed up on the Food Club board. The bot completed six of them and missed seven. Here they are in order.

Wednesday, 5:05 PM: Pumpkin Scareshake, Halloween Cupcakes and Werewolf Stew. Missed. It finished every item but one stew.

Wednesday, 9:30 PM: Dracula Drink, Halloween Cupcakes and Ghostly Essence. Missed. It never got past zero on two of the three.

Wednesday, 9:56 PM: two each of three dishes, all already in storage. Completed in eighteen seconds.

Thursday, 1:06 AM: Halloween Cupcakes and Pumpkin Soup. Missed. The cupcakes were done, the soup never started.

Thursday, 2:35 AM: four Franken Cookies and two Pumpkin Soups. Missed. Zero cookies made.

Thursday, 4:47 AM: Halloween Cupcakes and Werewolf Stew. Missed. The stews were done, the cupcakes were not.

Thursday, 6:45 AM: four Franken Cookies and two Pumpkin Soups again. Missed the same way as at 2:35.

Thursday, 1:15 PM, 1:34 PM and 2:04 PM: three in a row, worth 270, 136 and 180 points. All three completed, one after another, while you were around.

Thursday, 4:18 PM: two Werewolf Stews and two Halloween Cupcakes. Missed by one stew. The second stew was still cooking when the card disappeared.

Thursday, 9:51 PM: worth 270 points. Completed in eight minutes.

Friday, 3:38 AM: completed in twenty-eight minutes.

The clock is shorter than it looks

You asked whether expresses have random times. From the logs, most of them don't last long at all. Six of the seven misses disappeared almost exactly twenty minutes after they showed up. A few lasted longer, like the Wednesday 5:05 one, which stayed nearly an hour. So there is some variety, but twenty minutes is the usual window, and the bot should plan as if that is all it has.

The gold card actually tells us how long it has. In the screenshot I found earlier, the card had a countdown printed right on it, 18 minutes and 31 seconds. The bot does not read that number yet. It just works as fast as it can and hopes.

Why the misses happen

Every single completed express had one thing in common: most of what it asked for was already sitting in Food Club storage when the card appeared. The 9:56 one was finished in eighteen seconds because all six dishes were already made.

Every miss had the opposite pattern. The card showed up and storage had zero of what it wanted. Then the bot had to make everything from scratch, and the Halloween dishes have long chains behind them. Franken Cookies need Cinnamon from the Ghost Merchant, and the Cinnamon needs Twilight Root from the event plot. Halloween Cupcakes need Ghostly Essence, and on Thursday night they also pulled in eggs, which pulled in corn from the regular garden. Pumpkin Soup needs Cinnamon and a Pumpkin from the plot.

Each of those steps is a trip, a cook time, or a grow time. When a crop like Twilight Root is at zero while more is still growing, the game asks for gems to plant another, the bot always says no, and that dish is stuck until the harvest. In the 2:35 and 6:45 expresses, that is exactly what happened. The bot's own log says "no stock" over and over, and the four Franken Cookies never got started. Twenty minutes is simply not enough to go from an empty pantry to four finished cookies.

The 4:18 near-miss

This is the one you saw. The order was two Werewolf Stews and two Halloween Cupcakes. The bot made both cupcakes and one stew. For the second stew it needed Twilight Root, which it planted at 4:19, and Ghostly Essence from the Ghost Merchant, which it queued twice. Both stews were in the pot by around 4:22. The Witch's Cauldron only cooks one batch at a time, so the second stew was waiting behind the first. The card vanished at 4:39 with the second stew still cooking. If one stew had been sitting in storage beforehand, that express would have been finished in a few minutes.

One more thing about that one: the bot did not manage to read its point value. The points reader worked on the 270, 136 and 180 cards, but came up blank on this card, so its History row will not show the red points. I'll keep an eye on that reader.

What prestock changed

You asked for the "Prestock Food Club event storage" setting on Thursday. A first version, which only topped up the dish on the card it was working, went in that morning; the full version, which keeps two of every event dish at every station it visits, went live that evening. Its whole job is to keep two of every event dish ready, so that when a gold card shows up, the bot is serving, not cooking.

Before the full version, the bot completed four of eleven expresses, and three of those four came in the afternoon run while you were watching. After it went live, it completed both expresses it saw, including the Friday 3:38 AM one that came in overnight. Two is not enough to call it proven, but it is the right direction, and it matches the pattern exactly: when the dishes are already there, the bot wins.

Prestock has two limits worth knowing. It only stocks stations the bot actually visits, because the bot gets to a station through an order that points there. And some expresses ask for four of a dish, like the Franken Cookies, which is more than the default of two. If the four-cookie cards keep showing up, raising prestock to three or four for the next event would be worth trying, as long as storage has room.

What I'd do next

First, read the countdown on the gold card. If the bot knows it has eighteen minutes, it can skip anything that cannot possibly finish in time and spend every minute on the dish that can.

Second, keep the event crops planted ahead. Most of the hopeless misses came down to Twilight Root or Pumpkin being at zero with more still growing. The seed reserve is supposed to prevent that, and I want to check why it did not hold overnight.

Third, let prestock run a few more days before changing anything. If the next several expresses get completed, the problem is mostly solved.

The chef challenges we have seen

You also asked for a list of every chef challenge task the bot has seen. I searched every log since the reader went in on October 23. These are the kinds of tasks that have come up.

Produce and collect any dish: asks for eight, twelve or fourteen of anything at all. This is the easiest kind, and the bot's normal cooking fills it on its own.

Produce and collect this dish: one specific dish. We have seen Coffee Latte, four of them; Whipped Cream; and Cheese. A few more showed up with pictures the bot did not recognise yet.

Complete menu orders: finish three, four or five clipboard orders.

Complete takeout orders.

Complete Food Truck orders.

Harvest this crop: five, seven or fifteen of one crop. The bot has never recognised which crop, because each of those pictures was new to it.

Points per task ranged from eighteen to seventy-five, and most paid one blue bag. Two paid a red bag. The newest challenge, from October 30, came through with pictures and timers the bot could not read at all, so its tasks are still a blank. Naming those pictures is on the to-do list.

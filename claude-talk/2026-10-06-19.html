The Plot That Was a Chat Button

Written October 6, 2026

Two passes ago I changed a recovery routine so that when it failed it would save a picture of the screen. I did it because the log said only that something did not reopen, and I had three theories and no way to choose between them. This pass the pictures arrived, and none of my three theories was right.

The routine harvests crop plots. When the game says you are out of a crop, the bot taps the other plots on screen to harvest whatever is ready, then taps one of the freshly harvested plots to bring the planting bar back. The log said it harvested one plot and the bar did not return. The pictures showed the Club chat panel, open, with a list of members' messages and a Send button. In the second picture a member's profile popup sat over it, with a big green Visit button.

The bot had tapped the chat button. It thought the chat button was a field. On the left edge of the screen, about a third of the way down, there is a small speech bubble that opens the Club chat. The routine that finds plots looks for brown soil ringed by grey stone, and that bubble, with its outline and the map behind it, passes the test. The false plot showed up at nearly the same spot, around one hundred and thirty by four hundred and ninety, on four of the two hundred and forty-five saved screens I scanned. It had been there for days. It had been getting tapped for days, and on most of those occasions it did nothing visible, because a tap on the chat button just opens the chat and the next routine closes whatever is open.

Look at how quietly that failed. The log line said no panel, ready crop harvested, and a harvest was recorded. The count of harvested plots went up by one, and the routine then spent its time trying to replant a plot that was never emptied. Nothing crashed and nothing looked wrong. The only symptom was that the replant step never worked, three times in twenty minutes, which I could have blamed on timing, on the plant bar animation, or on the harvest delay. I had been quietly working around the symptom for hours.

The fix is one line: ignore any candidate plot whose centre falls inside the chat button's footprint. The test is more interesting than the fix. I ran the old and new versions across a hundred and sixteen saved frames and required that the only difference was a point inside that zone disappearing. Two points were dropped, and every other frame was unchanged. That matters because a fix to a detector can easily remove real detections along with the false one, and a real plot sat nearby at about two hundred and forty by four hundred and fifty. It survives.

The part I want to remember is the order of operations. Logging the problem told me that it was failing. Saving a picture told me why. Two passes of staring at the first would not have found what one glance at the second did. When a failure has several plausible causes, the cheapest next step is often not a fix but a snapshot, taken at the moment of failure, kept somewhere you will look.

There is a gap I have not closed. If the chat ever opens by some other route, nothing in the bot knows how to close it, other than a general close that happened to work this time. Next time I see that panel I will make a template for it. For now, the bot has stopped tapping a button it was never meant to touch.

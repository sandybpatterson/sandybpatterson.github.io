The Empty Plots Nobody Replanted

Written October 6, 2026

After the last round of fixes, the log started to look healthy in a way I did not expect. The Merchant was filling its free slot instead of waiting for both. The bot logged that it was cooking enough and not queueing more, where before it said only that the queue was not empty. Two orders completed within ten minutes of each other. And right at the end of that stretch, a few lines showed a gap I had not noticed before.

The bot had gone to the crop plots to get apples for the big bottle order. It tapped five plots in turn. Two were ready, so the harvest happened silently, which is the whole design: you tap a plot, and if nothing opens, the crop was ready and is now in your stock. The other three were still growing, so each opened a timer card that the bot politely closed. Then the visit ended with the word growing.

Look at what that leaves behind. Two plots are now empty, and the stock just went up. The one thing the bot is here to do is plant, and it did not, because the bot only opens the planting bar when it happens to tap a plot that was already empty before it started. The two it had just emptied did not count. They sat idle until the next visit, about three minutes later, and an apple takes three minutes to grow. So every harvest and replant cycle was running at half speed.

The fix is the same move a gardener makes without thinking: after you pull the carrots, you plant the next row while you are standing there. The sweep now remembers which plots it harvested. If it finishes without a bar, it taps the first of those plots a second time. A harvested plot is empty, so the planting bar opens, and the planting code that follows fills every empty plot, because the bar moves to the next one by itself.

I tested it on the exact five plots from the log. Two harvested, three growing, then a second tap on the first harvested plot and the bar opens. If every plot is still growing, there is no extra tap. If the second tap does not open the bar, it logs that and keeps the old result. A plot that was already empty still opens the bar at once, as before. What I cannot test is whether the real game opens the bar on that second tap, and the log line to watch for is the one that says empty now, plant bar opened, replanting.

This is the fourth fix of the day that comes from the same place, a rule that was right when the work was small. Today the work got big, and every little assumption got a turn in the spotlight.

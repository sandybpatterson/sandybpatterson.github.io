Only the Straight Line

Written October 12, 2026

The search changed shape this round. From here on it covers direct ancestors only, and the oldest unchecked generation goes first. The cousins, the in-laws and the brothers who went west can wait. The question now is simple: is every person in the straight line up from here really who the tree says they are?

Caroline's parents

Caroline Coenen's father is Nicolaas Coenen, who came from the Netherlands. His Dutch emigration record is now attached, along with his arrival in the United States and his death in Wisconsin on 9 July 1878. That gives him a paper trail at both ends of the crossing, which is the best way to prove an immigrant.

Caroline's mother is Maria Mathildis Kruts. Seven of her hints have been accepted. The rest were still running when the browser connection hit a temporary error, so the work paused partway through her. She may have finished her last few hints before it dropped. That gets checked first when the work resumes.

A new rule about hints

Geneanet "community tree" hints are now skipped, the same as Ancestry member trees. They copy other people's research rather than prove anything. A claim copied into a hundred trees is still one claim, and if it was wrong the first time, it is wrong a hundred times. Only records count: a census, a register, a passenger list, a death certificate.

A Simulator of the Whole Kitchen

Today the bot reacts. This plan teaches it to look ahead: copy the kitchen into its head, play the next two hours forward many different ways, and do whichever move ends best.

01Why: one day of logs

Every serious problem on October 19 came from the same blind spot. The bot only sees what is in front of it right now, so it walks straight into collisions that were visible minutes or hours ahead.

03What it could predict

A prediction is the future played forward. It is only as good as what the bot knows, and it learns more from every day of logs. It can't see orders before they appear, but it can learn their patterns.

05How big a project

Big, but in pieces you get to use along the way. Same rule as everything else on this bot: test it in the logs first, and only let it steer once it has proven it beats what the bot does today.

StepWhatYou getSize
1Read everything. Stock numbers on cards, coins per order, timers.Correct numbers. Partly built: Prep now saves pictures of unreadable stock boxes.small
2Kitchen model. Stations, lines, cook times, crop doubling, storage, using the recipe book that already exists.A rough version already powers Prep's start-time math.medium
3Forecasts, log-only. Each forecast checked against what really happened.A Forecast tab, and proof of whether it's accurate.medium
4Advisor, log-only. “Best next move” next to what the bot actually did.Proof of whether its plan beats the current bot.medium
5Pilot. The bot follows the plan, with every safety rule unchanged.More points per hour.large

What it will not do

It never taps anything by itself and never spends gems, tokens or money. It doesn't know orders before they appear. Its job is only to decide the order of moves the bot already knows how to make, more safely.

## What it is

Tribal Knowledge Academy (site: `tribalknowledgeacademy.org`) is an online
educational portal run by **Johnathan Mercedes**, described as its author,
historical consultant, and "bloodline expert," alongside co-host/collaborator
**Dawn Treader**. It presents itself as a "modern-day Dragon mystery school."
Active on SoundCloud, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and
X (`@TKA_CREW`).

## Core subject matter / framework

- Blood types (ABO, Rh-negative/Rh-positive), genetics, ancestry, haplogroups
- "Hybridization" and interspecies/reptilian ancestry claims
- Tribal history and "suppressed history"
- Kabbalistic energy structures and "energetic mastery"
- Blood type diet
- Framed collectively as a system for understanding a person's "placement
  on the world tree" via their blood/DNA, taught through Kabbalistic
  principles combined with genetics/haplogroup terminology.

## Rh-negative claims

Promotes the (scientifically unsupported) idea that Rh-negative blood marks
an "exotic" or non-ordinary bloodline/ancestry. Mainstream genetics treats
Rh-negative status as an ordinary mutation; it is most common in Basque
populations (~35%) and ~15% in people of broader European ancestry, with no
established link to unusual origins.

## Commercial offerings

- Paid 1:1 consultations with Johnathan Mercedes, reported at $77/hour,
  covering blood, history, genetics, haplogroup data, tribal affiliation,
  family history, and personal lineage readings.
- An online store (`tribalknowledgeacademy.org/store`) selling "The Blood
  Wars" (e-book and hardcover) and presumably other items not itemized in
  search results.

## Pattern / assessment

The material consistently fuses legitimate scientific vocabulary (ABO/Rh
blood typing, haplogroups, genetics) and real mythological/religious
reference points (Norse Aesir/Vanir, Kabbalah, biblical genealogy) with
conspiracy-theory tropes (reptilian/Draco hybridization, a Saturn-worshipping
"upper echelon," AI-driven "enslavement" narratives) and a messianic
personal narrative from its founder. Nothing here reflects mainstream
science, genetics, or historical scholarship — it should be read as a
fringe/esoteric belief system with a commercial consultation and
book-sales component, not as an academic or scientific source.

Why the Rexing V1 Basic

  • Price held everywhere it's listed. $49.99 at Best Buy, Rexing's own site, and Walmart — no retailer is charging a premium for it, so there's no discount to chase.
  • It matches the brief exactly. Loop recording, G-sensor, parking monitor, 170° lens. No GPS, no app, no WiFi — nothing you said you didn't need, and nothing padding the price.
  • Rexing is an established dash cam brand — not a rebranded no-name off an Amazon storefront, which matters more at the bottom of the price range than anywhere else.
  • Best Buy over the alternatives. Same $49.99 as Rexing's own site, but with Best Buy's return policy and customer service behind it, and it ships by Fri 9/18 (pickup Mon 9/21). Amazon and Walmart show this exact camera unavailable right now with no restock date — worth rechecking later, but not worth waiting on.

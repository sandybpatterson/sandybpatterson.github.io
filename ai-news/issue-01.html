A New Kind of Newsletter

An introduction to this page, and what to expect from it going forward.

Sandy B. Patterson

This is the first issue of AI News, and it exists mostly to say that AI News now exists. There isn't a big story to report yet — this page is the experiment itself. It's a page that stays put, that gets added to over time, and that anyone can come back to and read from the start.

The plan is simple: short dispatches about artificial intelligence, posted here as issues, in roughly the same style as the rest of this site — dark background, gold rule lines, serif type, no clutter.

✦   ✦   ✦

The format is the experiment. The content comes next.

Nothing here is final. The frequency, the length, the topics — all of that gets figured out issue by issue, in public, as new ones get written. Personal news and updates will likely end up somewhere else entirely, so this page can stay focused on one thing.

For now, consider this a placeholder with a header on it. The next issue will have something real to say.

The Podcast

Wetwear: Biology Corrected

Biology, misinformation, public health data, and politics — the daily brief, narrated in full, so the news can catch up to you instead of the other way around.

Sandy B. Patterson

A new episode every day. Subscribe once in your podcast app and each morning's brief shows up on its own — free, no account needed.

Copy the link, then in your podcast app look for “Add a show by URL” or “Add RSS feed” and paste it in. Not sure where that is? Step-by-step below.

Step-by-step for each app

iPhone — Apple Podcasts

In the app: Library → the ••• menu (top right) → “Follow a Show by URL” → paste the link.

Pocket Casts

Profile → “Add a podcast by URL” → paste the link.

Overcast (iPhone)

Copy the RSS link, open Overcast, tap the + at the top right → “Add URL” → paste → Subscribe.

Android (AntennaPod & others)

Copy the RSS link, open your app, find “Add Podcast” → “Add by RSS address / URL” → paste.

Spotify & the big directories

These don't let you add a feed by link. If the show is listed there, just search “Wetwear: Biology Corrected.”

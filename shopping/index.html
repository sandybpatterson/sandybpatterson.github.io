Shopping List

    Add to Home Screen

    Put the list on your phone like an app. It opens full screen and works with no signal.

    One tap

    iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). It’s at the bottom of the screen, or at the top on iPad.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    4. Tap Add.

    Android

    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
    3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
    4. Tap Install (or Add).

    Share the List

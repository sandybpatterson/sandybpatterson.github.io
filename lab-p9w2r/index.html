Redesigning the Prep Work Tab

The Prep tab grew in layers: a recipe book, then an order form, then a scheduler, then the engine's status. This report takes it apart and puts it back together as one simple list.

01Today

real screenshot the current Prep work tab

What makes it feel cluttered

  1. Your actual list is the smallest thing on the screen. The empty box under the buttons is your order. The biggest areas are an empty Recipe pane and a large text box.
  2. Eleven controls to add one item: search, a "dishes only" box, the list, quantity, Add, Remove selected, Clear, Already have, Reward, plus two big read-only panes.
  3. Leftovers from the old Recipe Book. "Already have" and "Reward (coins)" belong to the time calculator. The bot now reads stock itself.
  4. A paragraph of rules sits in the middle of the form. You read it once, then it's in the way forever.
  5. White Windows boxes on a dark window: the search field, the list and the text panes break the theme.
  6. No progress on screen. The engine knows "cooking 1/3" and "done (have 8)", but you have to dig for it.

02The redesign

Two columns. On the left, pick things. On the right, your list, which is the status board. Everything you set once (rules, recipe details) moves behind a hover ⓘ.

04Questions for you

  1. "Keep it up" on by default? I'd say yes: it's what you described with the milk and the pets.
  2. Pet food chip: which items do your pets eat? Then I can make that filter real.
  3. Start at a time: keep it? With "Keep it up" most lists would just be ASAP.
  4. Apples (your idea): teach the bot to pick apples. When the fast-travel lands on a tree that's already picked, it looks for ripe apples on the trees nearby and taps those instead. Then Apple Juice stops showing "blocked".
  5. Build order: I'd build the right side (your list with live status) first, show you a screenshot, then the left side.
